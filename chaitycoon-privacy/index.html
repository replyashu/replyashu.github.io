Chai Tycoon Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Chai Tycoon: Idle Food Empire ("the app") is an idle game made by Ashutosh Sharma. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (cash, stalls, managers, upgrades, fame, gems and streak) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location based on IP address, and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

Learn how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings under Google → Ads.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app sends local reminders when your cash box is full or your daily reward is ready. This is created on your device and uses no personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
